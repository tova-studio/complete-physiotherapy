About us

About Complete Physiotherapy

Complete Physiotherapy is a locally owned and operated practice. We provide patients with two experienced clinicians with over 24 years of experience in treating a wide range of conditions.

At Complete Physiotherapy you will be welcomed into a small and professional service that has the expertise to guide you through your treatment with confidence. We operate out of the Complete Podiatry premises and have an attached Pilates studio and exercise equipment to support you with your complete rehabilitation needs.

Good to know

Your questions, answered

Is physiotherapy funded by ACC?

Complete Physiotherapy offers physiotherapy privately or as funded by ACC. Let us know which applies to you when you get in touch, and we'll go from there.

Where is Complete Physiotherapy located?

We're at 3/19 Gerald Street, Lincoln. We operate out of the Complete Podiatry premises, with an attached Pilates studio and exercise equipment.

Which areas do you serve?

We provide a high-quality, comprehensive physiotherapy service to Lincoln and the wider Selwyn area, just out of Christchurch.

Who will I see for treatment?

The practice is owned by physiotherapist Jacquie Webb. You'll be welcomed into a small, professional service with two experienced clinicians who have over 24 years of experience.

How do I book an appointment?

Phone us on 03 366 2550 or email completephysiotherapylincoln@gmail.com and we'll arrange a time that suits you.

Do you offer more than physiotherapy?

Yes. As well as physiotherapy, we offer Pilates classes and cancer rehabilitation to support your complete rehabilitation needs.
